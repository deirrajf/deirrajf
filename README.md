## 👋 Hi, I'm Deirra

I'm Deirra, a network security engineer and content creator with 16+ years of experience designing and securing the networks that keep businesses connected. My background spans routing and switching, data center networking, and network security, with deep roots in Cisco ISE and identity-based access.

These days I'm focused on where secure networks meet AI: segmentation, identity, and Zero Trust, plus how agentic AI can be built and secured for the enterprise. You can follow what I'm learning on my [blog](https://deirrafootman.substack.com).

## 🔐 What I focus on

- **Network security & segmentation:** policy, NAC, and getting traffic where it should (and only where it should) go
- **Identity & Zero Trust:** identity-driven access as the foundation, not the afterthought
- **AI security:** risk and compliance for the AI tools organizations are rushing to adopt
- **Agentic AI & workflows:** practical agents that triage, document, and deliver alongside engineers

## 📚 What I'm learning

- **AI solutions architecture:** designing and shipping AI systems that hold up in the enterprise
- **Agentic AI security:** how to secure agents, their tools, and the data they touch
- **AI/Agentic Ops:** putting agents to work in operations to triage, document, and report alongside engineers

*I share what I learn along the way on [Engineering the Loop](https://deirrafootman.substack.com).*

## 🧠 Side project: Cortex & Code

Beyond the packets and prompts, I'm curious about what technology is doing to *us*. On [Cortex & Code](https://cortexandcode.substack.com/), I explore technical and digital philosophy: how AI and the tools we build are reshaping attention, identity, work, and what it means to be human.
